Tacklebox

The tacklebox is a menu accessed by dropping your rod and selecting the tacklebox button. Baits & lures can be placed inside the tacklebox to be used while fishing (they are used up from top left to bottom right).

Bait Packages

Bait packages are obtained from location leveling, collection completion, contests, power rewards, and voting. Advanced Bait Packages include specific baits that cannot be found in Standard Bait Packages. The rarity of the bait packages determines the rarity distribution of their contents.

Bait

Baits are single-use items. Bait can be specific to Freshwater, Saltwater, a specific location or they can be universal. They can grant bonuses and debuffs for Bite Speed, Fish Likelihood & Fish weight/length.

Stacking & Splitting bait

When you have multiple of the same bait, grab one of the baits and right-click it onto the other to stack it in your inventory or tacklebox.
When you want to split bait, hold the desired bait in your hand and type /splitter <amount> to split your bait into your desired amount.

Lures

Lures have multiple uses (ranging from 20-50, depending on the lure). Unlike baits, they cannot be stacked together. Lures can be specific to freshwater or saltwater, or they can be universal. Most types of lures come in various sizes, which influences the bonuses granted. Lures grant bonuses/debuffs for Bite Speed, Fish Likelihood & Fish Size. And some can grant increased Variant Chance.

Fish Likelihood

Certain baits and lures have a Fish Likelihood attribute.


Based on the bait or lure being used when the fish is determined, if you are confirmed for likelihood by being within the “likelihood range” with your random integer (between 1 and 100), then one of the following things may happen:


Option 1: You’ve pulled a rarity that has a fish of the likelihood group in it. If it has multiple, it selects a random one. This results in you catching one of the targeted fish.


Option 2: You’ve pulled a rarity that does not have the likelihood group in it. If this is the case, then the outcome is as follows:


2a: If the rarity is a higher rarity, then it will cycle to a lower rarity until said rarity has a fish in the likelihood group. Then it will lock it in.


2b: if rarity gets to a common after cycling downwards without a fish while not going through every single rarity already, OR is a common from the start, then it will cycle through higher rarities descending in something called the “likelihood retribution”. This is not guaranteed unless 100% likelihood. There is a distinct formula for this part for each rarity to make sure it’s evenly distributed per rarity.


2c: If cycled through both of these without selecting a fish, then it will just give you a random fish of the original rarity, like normal fishing behaviour.


Note: the system also automatically knows what rarities have the fish in them because it filters all the fish out after confirming the likelihood group, so it only goes through the ones it’s pulled
