Fishing Menu

Opening your fishing fishing menu with the book in your 9th hotbar slot, you will open the fishing menu. In the fishing menu there are a few buttons:

Fishing Menu

Image Name Information
Daily Cosmetic Store Here you can view your daily cosmetics,
purchase credits to buy skins.
Can be rerolled with ranks.
RecipesHere you can view custom item recipes.
Fish CompendiumHere you can view your personal fishing codex,
view fish data and location leveling info.
QuestsHere you can view you quests in the location you are at,
also done by doing /quests.
Turbo TravelTravel to a location from everywhere, requires atleast to use this, otherwise use the locations NPC.
CosmeticsHere you can view all your unlocked & locked cosmetics.
CrewsHere you can view info about your current crew.
ShopHere you can purchase blocks to build with on your crew island.
CraftingHere you can access a workbench to craft with building blocks bought from the shop, as well as your unlocked Recipes.
Player profileHere you can access your profile to view you current
stats, equipped items, collections, achievements
and profile displays.
HelpHere you can open the /help screen and see lots on information about the game systems.
SettingsHere you can toggle the side Scoreboard and Ambient sounds on or off.
Daily MissionsHere you can view your Daily Missions and your progress towards completing them.
