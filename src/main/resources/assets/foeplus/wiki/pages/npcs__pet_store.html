The Pet Store

The Pet Store, located to the east of spawn (to the left), is an NPC used to sell unwanted pets that are below excellent for money. The higher the rating and rarity, the more money you receive.

The formula for the sale price of a pet is as follows: 

Price = (5000*2^[10*{Rating-0.7}])*Rarity

Where Rating is a decimal (e.g. 50% is 0.5), and Rarity is how many commons merge into it. So Common = 1, Rare = 2, Epic = 4, Legendary = 8, and Mythic = 16.
