Auction House

All players have access to the auction house with the command /ah. On the auction house, players can list the items they want to sell.

To list an item on the auction house, hold the item in your hand and do /ah sell <sell price>. When listing an item on the auction house, you will have to pay a 2% tax on the selling price of the item. A listed item will be on the auction house for 24 hours. If not sold, you can retrieve it by doing /overflow.
