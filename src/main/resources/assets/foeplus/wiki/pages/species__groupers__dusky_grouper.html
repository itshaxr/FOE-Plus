A grouper found in the Eastern Atlantic around the coasts of Africa as well as in the Mediterranean Sea. Also found in some parts of the Southwestern Atlantic along southeastern Brazil. Reaches up to 132.5 pounds, 59 inches.

Found at Mediterranean Sea.
