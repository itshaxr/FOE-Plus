Quests

Quests are missions you activate per location that give rewards for catching fish based on size or rarity.


When starting a quest you will be asked to select either an EASY, MEDIUM or HARD quest, and then you select whether you want the quest to be for Adult, Large, Rare, Epic or Legendary fish (EASY quests can not be for Legendary fish). The amount of fish you need to catch as well as the rewards you get scale with both player level and location level.


You access the Quest Menu by going into the Fishing Menu and clicking quests. You can also access it by typing /quests, /quest or /q. Once there, click on an open quest slot and choose the tier of quest you want to start.


Everyone starts with 3 quest slots. The only way to increase this is by purchasing ranks. Each rank increases the number of slots by 1, up to a total of 8.


You can abandon the quest you are given by clicking on the active quest slot and choosing abandon. This costs some money but can be beneficial in order to speed up questing, as you might get an easier quest to complete.


There are three tiers of quests, each giving better rewards than the last. The difficulty of quests increase with level.
