A jack found in the Western Atlantic from Massachusetts, USA to Brazil and in the Eastern Atlantic from from Côte d'Ivoire to Angola. It is also found throughout the Indo-Pacific and the Eastern Pacific from California, USA to Equador. Reaches up to 102 pounds, 71 inches.

Found at Hawaii, Cairns.
