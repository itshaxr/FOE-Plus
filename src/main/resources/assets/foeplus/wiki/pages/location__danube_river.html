Danube River

Danube River is the 9th unlockable location of FishOnMC. This location is the second-longest river in Europe with a length of 1,770 miles. Danube passes trough 10 countries, this is more than any other river worldwide.

Real Life History

Danube River is a great romping ground for fishermen, it has long gravel bars that offer Danube’s fish sufficient
opportunities to lay eggs, and side-arms of the Danube
extending into authentic river wetlands.

Fishing in the Danube River still holds an important economic value and provides loads of cultural activity. However,
commercial fishing on the Danube has gotten less popular due to overfishing. Local fishermen continue to fish in the Danube River.
