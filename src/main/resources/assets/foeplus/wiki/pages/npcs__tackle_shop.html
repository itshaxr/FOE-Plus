Tackle Shop

The Tackle Shop

The Tackle Shop NPC is located in the Tackle Shop within the Bass Pro Shop behind spawn. At the Tackle Shop, you can buy up to 7 unique baits or lures. These are refreshed every 4 hours, including the quantities of the available tackle. The price of the bait scales based on quantity and rarity.

Bait & Lure Rarities

When the shop refreshes, it will sell new baits or lures. The rarity of the tackle is determined at the time of the refresh. The chances are as follows:

60% 28.9% 10% 1% 0.1%

Variant lures are possible to obtain through the tackle shop.

Quantity of Tackle
