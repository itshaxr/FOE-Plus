Cosmetics

There are six types of cosmetics in FishOn: TAGS, ROD SKINS, MINIGAME GRAPHICS, TRAILS, HATS & ROD TITLES. There are many ways to obtain cosmetics.

Daily Cosmetic Store

The Daily Cosmetic Store is a store that can be accessed at spawn, through the /store command or through the Fishing Menu. Certain ROD SKINS and HATS can be purchased here for credits. This store
refreshes every 24 hours at 12:00am UTC. Some ranks give the player the ability to reroll the store a certain amount of times per day.

Ranks

Certain ROD SKINS and all TRAILS are unlocked through having ranks. The higher the rank, the more
cosmetics unlocked.

Location Levels

Each location unlocks a ROD TITLE at Level 10, as well as a ROD SKIN at level 15. Cypress Lake also grants an additional ROD TITLE at Level 2.

Compendiums

Completing normal fish compendiums unlocks a TAG for each location.

Events

Limited Time Events grant many cosmetic rewards including TAGS, ROD SKINS, MINIGAME GRAPHICS, HATS and ROD TITLES.

Collections

Obtaining level 7 collections for each variant unlocks a trident themed ROD SKINS.
