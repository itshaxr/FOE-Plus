Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

You can use the command '/tasks' to see a quick tutorial which will give you the rundown on the basics of the gameplay loop.

How do I identify my armor?

To identify your armor, Head to the spawn and look left. The Identifier is located to the left, right behind the Help NPC. Once at the Identifier, right click the NPC and click the armor piece you would like to identify, and then click the Ender Eye to identify your armor piece.

How do I craft new rod parts?

To craft new rod parts, Head to the Artisan. The Artisan is located at spawn right behind the cosmetics on the left side. Once at the Artisan, right-click the NPC and click an empty minecart. Click the rod part you would like to craft, and it should show you the Build Requirements and the time it will take to craft the part.

How do I use my Bait/Lure?

To use your Bait/Lure, right-click your rod in your inventory (or hold your rod in your hand and press your drop key) and click Tackle Box. Once you click Tackle Box, you can put the Bait/Lure in, and it will be used starting from Left to Right.

How do I tier up my armor?

To tier up your armor, head to the Forger at spawn. The Forger is located behind the Identifier and to the right of the Scrapper. Once at the Forger right click the NPC and click the armor piece you wish to tier up, once you click the armor piece hover over the axe and it will show you the requirements to tier up said armor piece, once ready and meet the requirements click the axe and your armor piece will tier up to the next level.
