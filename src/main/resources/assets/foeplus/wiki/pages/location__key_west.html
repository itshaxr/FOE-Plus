Key West

Key West is the 6th unlockable and first Saltwater location of FishOnMC.
This location has long been shaped by pirates, shipwrecks, and marine legends. The stories of the sea, passed down for centuries, make its waters
more mysterious than ever.

Real Life History

Native tribes had fished here long before anyone else. However, Cuban
fishermen established their fishing camps in the 1700s. In the
1800s, pirates salvaged cargo from lost ships. Around the 1930s, deep-sea fishing sports were popularized, and this remains a tradition that continues to this day.

Today, Key West remains a vital fishing hub, with fishing under the Florida Keys National Marine Sanctuary to protect its waters.
