Fishing

Fishing is the core of the game. As a result, there are many custom features that have been added in relation to fishing.

Fishing Minigame

Fishing Minigame

Tutorial

Sneak to increase line tension (make the blue bar go to the right). STOP sneaking to make it go down (left). When the green progress bar at the top is full, you have caught the fish!

When the blue bar reaches the start or the end of the minigame, your fish will snap off.

Tension Bar

The blue bar below represents how the fish is fighting. Higher rarity fish will pull harder, thus making them more difficult to catch. If this bar reaches the end on either side, the line will snap and the fish will be lost. This bar is unaffected by the size of the fish.


Progress

The green bar above represents how close you are to catching the fish. Think of this bar like the health meter of the fish. Once you get this bar to 100%, the fish is caught. The weight of a fish directly affects how much progress is needed to reel in the fish. This bar is affected by natures and fishing stats, namely reel speed and line strength.

Fish Sizes

Fish have different sizes that affect their sell price and XP. You can increase the chance for bigger fish with Scale. When the size tier of a fish is generated, it will then generate a length from within that tier's range for the specific fish. The weight is then determined from the neighborhood of that length. After this, any size bonuses from lures, armor rolls, or location rolls are added together and multiplied by the generated weight and length.
