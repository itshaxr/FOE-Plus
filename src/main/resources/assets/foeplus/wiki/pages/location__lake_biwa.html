Lake Biwa

Lake Biwa is the 3rd unlockable location of FishOnMC. This location is the largest freshwater lake in Japan. It is also one of the world's oldest lakes, making it a site of great ecological and historical significance.

Real Life History

Lake Biwa has long been a vital freshwater fishing hub in Japan, with traditions dating back over a thousand years. The fishermen who reside here form a close-knit community that has passed down fishing techniques for generations.
