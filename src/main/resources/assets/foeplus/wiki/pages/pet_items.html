Pet Items

Pet items are craftable items which can be equipped to pets by right clicking the pet in your inventory. They increase the stats of the pet and can give it added effects. Only one pet item can be active on a pet. Their crafting recipes can be viewed in the recipes tab within the Fishing Menu.
