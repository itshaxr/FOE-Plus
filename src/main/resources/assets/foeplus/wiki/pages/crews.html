Crews

Crews are player-based groups that you can create or join. With a crew, you gain access to a private island that can be accessed by /c go. You also get a Money and XP bonus (0.5% base) for fishing next to one of your crew members within a 10 block range.

The command prefix for crews is /crew and can be shortened to /c. Here is a list of every crew command:

Command Function
/c create <name> Creates a crew, you must not be in a crew and have $15000 to create a crew. Crew names must be unique.
/c invite <player> Invites a player to your crew; the player must not already be in a crew.
/c join <crew name> Joins a crew; you must have received an invite to use this command.
/c chat Toggles crew chat mode, messages that you send are in a channel with your crew members and not with the public.
/c kick <player> Kicks a specified player from your crew. Crew Rank Lieutenant or Captain is required.
/c delete Disbands and deletes your crew. Crew Rank Captain is required.
/c [promote/demote] <player> Raises or lowers a player's rank by 1 from your crew. Works only going between Crew Rank Member and Lieutenant. The ranks in a crew go from Member > Lieutenant > Captain.
/c info <player/crew name> Shows the crew's information. Same functionality with both /c stats <player/crew name> and /c.
/c sethome Changes the crew island's spawn and looking direction.
/c visit <crew name> Visits a crew that isn't yours.
/c [go/home] Teleports you to your crew island.
/c lock Toggles your crew island from being visited or not. Crew Rank Lieutenant or Captain is required.
/c fly Toggles flying in your crew island. Members and Visitors can use this command.
/c leave Leave your current crew.
/c captain <player> Promotes a player to Crew Rank Captain. Crew Rank Captain is required.
/cc <message> Will send you message in crew chat without toggling it.

Leveling

Crew missions are reset every day and give a large bonus to your crew leveling. Your crew xp additionally increases as you fish. Crew missions are the same for each crew on a given day.

Mission Minimum Maximum XP

100 150 +25K

2 2 +50K
1 1 +50K
Gigantic 3 5 +50K
Fish Group 75 150 +40K

Every day, crews are assigned 1 Total mission, 3 Rarity missions, 1 Variant/Gigantic Mission, and 2 Fish Group Missions.

Leveling increases the island border by 5 blocks per level up to level 20, another 5 blocks at level 25, and the last 5 blocks at level 30, for a total of 200×200 at crew level 30.

The Money and XP bonus is increased by 0.5% every level up to level 10 for a maximum bonus of 5%.

Every 5 levels, a new member slot is unlocked until the crew reaches 10 total members.
