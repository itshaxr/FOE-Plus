List of NPCs

Appraiser

N/A

Artisan

The Artisan lets you upgrade your rod parts.

Blacksmith

N/A

Calibrator

The Calibrator located at the Bass Pro Shop behind spawn is where you calibrate your rod parts.

Cosmetic Store

Located at Cypress Lake's Spawn, the Cosmetic Store NPC brings up the Daily Cosmetic Store.

Crews

Located at Cypress Lake's Spawn, the Crews NPC brings up your Crew info.

Daily Reward

N/A

Fish Merchant

The Fish Merchant is where you sell your fish. Check out each location's page for their locations.

Forge

The Forge is where you upgrade both armor tiers and supercharges tiers.

Help

Located at Cypress Lake's Spawn, the Help NPC brings up the /help screen.

Identifier

The Identifier gives unidentified armor its quality.

Instance Selector

Located at Cypress Lake's Spawn, the Instance Selector NPC brings up the /instances menu which lets you change between the 6 instances.

Locations

The Locations NPC lets you travel between the various locations on the server. Check out each location's page for their locations.

Pet Store

The Pet Store lets you sell unwanted pets that are below excellent for money.

Scrapper

The Scrapper lets you scrap unwanted common armor.

Tackle Shop

The Tackle Shop located at the Bass Pro Shop behind spawn, here you can buy baits and lures every 4 hours.
