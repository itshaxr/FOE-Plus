Toledo Bend Reservoir

Toledo Bend Reservoir is the 7th unlockable location of FishOnMC.
This location is the largest man-made body of water and has an area of 185,000 acres.

Real Life History

Created in the 1950s, Toledo Bend reservoir became a top fishing spot for largemouth bass, with this new lake and its great environment, creating
almost perfect fishing conditions.

The fishery gained massive popularity around the 1990s, when the association began releasing more than 30 million bass into this massive reservoir. With great fishing and well-designed facilities, Toledo Bend has around 100 large regional and national fishing tournaments every year. Attracting thousands of
fishermen from across the country.
