Power

Power is earned as you progress through the server, unlocking rewards and the ability to use certain gear.

You can check your Power by going to your /stats page and hovering over the Treble Hook Icon ().

Earning Power

You can earn Power in 8 different ways:

Compendium Completion Varies per location
