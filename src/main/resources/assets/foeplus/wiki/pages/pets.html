Pets

Pets are different animals from each location that you can catch and level up. Every location has a custom, location specific pet that can be used for a stat buff. Each pet has a rarity and a rating attached to it, which makes every caught pet unique. Only one pet can be equipped at once, whichever pet is equipped, will the one gaining levels while you fish.

All pets come with 4 random stat boosts, 2 for scale as well as 2 for luck. One set of luck and scale that's climate bound, and another set that is location bound. For a common pet each extra stat will be ranged from 5 to 25, meaning a perfect common would have 25 on every stat.

Pets Menu

The pets menu can be accessed by right clicking on a pet while it is in the inventory. It has four uses: Pet skins, Pet Items, Pet Trails and Pet Merging.

Pet Merging

Pet merging allows you to combine two pets of the same rarity once they have both reached level 100 to form a new pet which is one rarity higher. Each stat is averaged between the two pets.

Obtaining Pets

Following the tutorial in /tasks will reward a guaranteed pet drop. The other ways to earn a pet are from Hard Quests, which have a 1/16 chance to drop a common pet when completed, and Contests with 3 or more people in when you place in the top 3. All other pets will be found as drops while catching fish, with the chances for each rarity found in the table below.

Selling Pets

Pets below excellent can be sold for money at the Pet Store. All Pets can be sold to other players, either on the auction house (/ah) or via /trade.
