Cypress Lake

Cypress Lake is the spawn location of FishOnMC. This location is home to the town of Cypress Lake, which serves as the main village of the server.
Many important NPCs are hosted here and have visible homesteads.

Real Life History

Caddo Lake, the inspiration of Cypress Lake, was first inhabited by Spanish and
French explorers. Around the 1800s, they built ceremonial centers and
maintained far-reaching trade routes they used to trade fish they had caught. Spreading across 27,000 acres, Caddo Lake is a maze of waterways and
wetlands, protecting and nurturing hundreds of unique species.
