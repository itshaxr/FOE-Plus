Cape Cod

Cape Cod is the 13th unlockable location of FishOnMC. This location offers great fishing opportunities for both recreational and sport fishermen, the rich fishing grounds and diverse ecosystems of Cape Cod make it a popular fishing destination in the United States.

Real Life History

Cape Cod is rich in cold storage, piers, shipbuilders, and saltworks.

Due to overfishing, catching groundfish and cod decreased by 90%. Even with this decline in groundfish and cods, the locals adapted, and to this day, Cape Cod remains a great fishing location.
