Fishing Stats

Reel Speed

Reel Speed affects the amount of progress earned per successful tick in the fishing minigame. The base value is 0.6 “units” of progress. 1 Reel Speed is equivalent to 0.001 units, so +600 would be needed to double the base speed. Reel Speed is increased by upgrading your Reel.

Bite Speed

Bite Speed affects the wait time from when the bobber hits the water to when the fish's particles spawn in the water. The maximum wait time (20s by default) is displayed in your stats profile, and the minimum wait time is a quarter of the max. 1 Bite Speed is equal to 0.02 seconds off the max wait time and 0.005 seconds off the min wait time. The minimum wait time caps out at 1.5 seconds, and the maximum wait time caps out at 3 seconds. Bite Speed is increased by upgrading your Pole.

Line Strength

Line Strength affects the total progress needed to fill the fishing minigame bar. As Line Strength increases, the total progress needed is reduced, resulting in a faster catch time. Line Strength is increased by upgrading your line.

Luck

Luck increases the chance of catching higher rarity fish. Luck can be increased with Armor, Pets, and one of the generic location rolls. The rare rainbow event also provides +500 Luck for a short period.
The luck formula is as follows:
Rarity % Chance (0–2749 Luck) % Chance (2750–4722 Luck) % Chance (4723+ Luck)
Common 55 - (Luck * 0.02) 0% 0%
Rare 30 + (Luck * 0.002) 85 - (Luck * 0.018) 0%
Epic 10 + (Luck * 0.004) 10 + (Luck * 0.004) 95 - (Luck * 0.014)
Legendary 4 + (Luck * 0.006) 4 + (Luck * 0.006) 4 + (Luck * 0.006)
Mythical 1 + (Luck * 0.008) 1 + (Luck * 0.008) 1 + (Luck * 0.008)

Note: Any probability below 0% means that rarity can't be caught.

Scale increases the chance of catching higher size tier fish. Scale can be increased with Armor, Pets, and one of the generic location rolls.
The scale formula is as follows:

Prospect

Prospect increases the chance of getting shards from fishing. The base chance is 1/50. The formula for Prospect is Shard chance = 1/(50/[1 + {Prospect * 0.0008}])

Bonus XP

Bonus XP multiplies the amount of player XP, location XP, and crew xp received when catching a fish. This is a separate multiplier from 2x XP Boosters.

Bonus Money

Bonus Money multiplies the amount of money received when catching a fish. This is a separate multiplier from 2x Money Boosters. It's important to note that this boost is only applied when the fish is caught, not sold.

Pet Luck

Pet Luck increases the chance to catch a pet.

The formula is as follows:
Rarity Formula
1/(750/[1 + Pet Luck/100])
1/(4000/[1 + Pet Luck/100])
1/(10000/[1 + Pet Luck/100])
1/(30000/[1 + Pet Luck/100])
1/(80000/[1 + Pet Luck/100])


So if you had +100% pet luck your odds for a mythic pet would go from 1/80000 to 1/40000.
