Fishing Events

There are two types of events within FishOn: Reoccurring Events & Limited Time Events.

Reoccurring Events

Weather Events

Rain Shower

A Rain Shower can start randomly every 1 to 2 hours and lasts between 450 and 600 seconds. It gives +50 Bite Speed.
While it is raining, the weather icon at the top of the screen turns to a ☂ symbol.

Thunderstorms and Supercells

Thunderstorms spawn 1 in 3 times when it starts to rain and grants +100 bite speed as well as a 1/500 chance to fish up a Lightning in a Bottle. These last between 300 and 680 seconds.

A Supercell is a buffed version of the Thunderstorm and happens 1 in 15 times that it starts to rain. This storm gives +150 bite speed and the chance for a Lightning in a Bottle is 1/250. These last between 250 and 540 seconds.

While a storm is active, the weather icon at the top of the screen turns to a ⚡︎ symbol.

Rainbows

Rainbows have a 1 in 4 chance to spawn after it stops raining granting +500 luck and lasts betwen 180 and 420 seconds.

Moon Events

Moon Events occur every full moon (Once every 8 Minecraft days or 160 minutes) and last for 600 seconds.

Unique Events

Unique Events happen every 1 to 2 hours.

Gold Rush

The Gold Rush Event changes the base rate of climate shards from 1/50 to 1/25. It also enables the chance to fish up the Prospecting Amulet at a 1/1000 chance. The Gold Rush lasts for between 900 and 960 seconds.

While the Gold Rush is active, the weather icon at the top of the screen turns to a ⚠ symbol.

Blooming Oasis

The Blooming Oasis Gives +100% pet luck for between 900 and 960 seconds.

While the Oasis is active, the weather icon at the top of the screen turns to a ♣ symbol.

Fabled Event

The Fabled Event happens twice per day, enabling Fabled Variant fish to be caught at a 1/3000 chance in a location declared in chat. Only one person per fabled event can catch the Fabled Fish.

While the Fabled Event is active, the weather icon at the top of the screen turns to a ★ symbol.

Location Specific Events

Cryptid Sighting at Cypress Lake

When fishing at Cypress Lake between the in game hours of 1am and 3am, there is a 1/300 chance that Bigfoot will steal your fish. This means that instead of catching a fish, you instead get either a Bigfoot Tooth or a Bigfoot Fur. These can be used to craft some pet items.




Limited Time Events

Limited-time events happen seasonally and are each unique. They grant many rewards for reaching thresholds and advancing on the leader board.
Completing event milestones would reward limited cosmetics.

Winter Event 2025

The 2025 winter event started on 02/22/2025 and lasted until 03/01/2025.
The event revolved around catching a new fish variant called fish.
Every salmon in Kenai River had a 1/50 base chance to be a variant.

Sawfish Event 2025

The 2025 Sawfish event started on 04/19/2025 and lasted until 04/26/2025.
The event revolved around catching the Smalltooth Sawfish.
The base chance of catching one of these notorious fish was 1/100 when catching any fish in Key West. Using the Stone crab bait increased your chances of catching this fish.

Meanmouth Bass Event 2025

The 2025 Meanmouth Bass event started on 06/28/2025 and lasted until 07/06/2025.
The event revolved around catching the Meanmouth Bass in the Great Lakes.
The base chance of catching one of these hybrid fish was 1/40 whenever you would have caught either a Smallmouth Bass or a Largemouth Bass at Great Lakes.

Shark Week Event 2025

The 2025 Shark Week Event started on 07/20/2025 and lasted until 07/26/2025.
The event revolved around catching the Common Remora and a new fish variant called sharks.
The base chance of catching a Common Remora was 1/50 on any catch in Key West, Cape Cod or Cairns.
The chance of a Shark being an variant was 1/50.

Halloween Event 2025

The 2025 Halloween Event started on 10/31/2025 and lasted until 11/07/2025.
The event revolved around catching a new fish variant called fish.
Every fish in Cypress Lake had a 1/50 base chance to be a variant, With the Creepy Crawlers Bait increasing the odds.

Christmas Event 2025

The 2025 Christmas Event started on 12/27/2025 and lasted until 01/03/2026.
The event revolved around catching a new fish variant called fish.
Every fish had a 1/50 base chance to be a variant, With the Frozen Crawlers Bait increasing the odds.

Hanami Event 2026

The 2026 Hanami Event started on 04/11/2026 and lasted until 04/18/2026.
The event revolved around catching Koi fish in Lake Biwa.
When you caught a Common Carp in Lake Biwa, you had a chance to instead reel in a Koi.
The odds were: 1/20 for Kohaku Koi, 1/50 for Showa Koi, and 1/200 for Tancho Koi.

Shark Week Event 2026

The 2026 Shark Week Event started on 07/26/2026 and lasted until 08/02/2026.
The event revolved around catching the Great Hammerhead.
The base chance of catching a Great Hammerhead was 1/80 on any catch in Key West or Cairns With the Southern Ray Cutbait Bait increasing the odds.
