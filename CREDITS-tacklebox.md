# tacklebox credit

Parts of FOE+ come from **tacklebox** by **landofif (dofi)** (Minecraft: merryfish):
https://github.com/landofif/tacklebox, used with their permission under the MIT License below.

From tacklebox:
- The big catch moment: the soft glow at the edges of your screen when you catch a big one
  (`handler/renderer/BigCatchMoment.java`, HUD settings → Big Catch Moment) and the big catch sounds
  (`handler/store/NotifySounds.java`).
- The water rings at your bobber and on a catch (`handler/renderer/WaterRings.java`, Renderer → Water Rings).
- The see-through fishing line colour (`helper/LineColour.java`).
- The idea for the dry streak lines in chat (shards, Prospecting Amulets).

They were rewritten for FOE+ (Mojang mappings, Fzzy Config, FOE+'s own settings).

landofif also wrote the bait sorter, ported from FOECollab: see [CREDITS-FOECollab.md](CREDITS-FOECollab.md).
